Guidelines

1 Same event: both mainly report the same specific happening (same occurrence, actors and time), whatever the angle or language. 2 Same story: same ongoing issue but different happenings (another day's development, a reaction that is itself the news, a follow-up). 3 Unrelated: different issues, even with a shared person or place. 0 Unsure: too little text or genuinely ambiguous.
