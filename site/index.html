News value · Logistic regression

What makes a story news

Whether an outlet covers an event, modelled from how the event looked when it first broke. Each dot multiplies the odds of coverage; right of the line raises them. Filled dots are clear effects.

Corrections and clarifications

Headlines changed after publishing

Feeds are read every four hours. When a story comes back under a new headline, both versions are kept. Spacing, case and quote marks are ignored.

Lost in translation

Where English and Hindi told different stories

For stories both languages covered, how far apart the English and Hindi articles sit in a multilingual embedding space.

Distance: 1 minus the cosine of the mean BGE-M3 vectors per language. Events with 2+ outlets in each language.

About

Nazariya models how India's news outlets cover the same events. It measures coverage patterns, not truth or intent, and every estimate carries uncertainty. Source code

Data
  • Outlet RSS feeds and news sitemaps: headlines, times, links (list and status)
  • PIB: government press releases
  • Wikidata: people, parties and states named in articles
  • Census of India 2011: state populations
