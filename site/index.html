How India's newsrooms actually work.

Every article from 38 English and Hindi outlets, matched into events across languages, then modelled: which outlets behave alike, who sets the agenda and who copies, what makes a story news, which stories get ignored, and how long they stay alive.

About

Nazariya models how India's news outlets cover the same events. It measures coverage patterns, not truth or intent, and every estimate carries uncertainty; small samples are marked. Source code

Data
  • Outlet RSS feeds and news sitemaps: headlines, times, links (list and status)
  • PIB: government press releases
  • Wikidata: people, parties and states named in articles
  • Census of India 2011: state populations
