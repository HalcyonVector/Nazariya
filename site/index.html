Articles are grouped into events automatically: multilingual sentence embeddings (bge-m3) of each headline and opening paragraph, clustered online with a similarity threshold. Groupings can be wrong. Names under each headline are people, parties and institutions linked to Wikidata with a published alias table; links can be wrong too. "Framed as" shows the angle each outlet took (Media Frames Corpus categories), predicted by a model trained on LLM labels: provisional, roughly 65% agreement with a stronger reference model, not yet checked against human labels. Each strip shows only whether an outlet carried the story, not how it framed it.

We store headlines, links and derived data only, never article text. Source code and evaluation reports: github.com/HalcyonVector/Nazariya.
